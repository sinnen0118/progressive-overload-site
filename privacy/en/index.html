Progress Note Workout Log & Program Manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This English version is provided for convenience. If it differs from the Japanese version, the Japanese version prevails. (Japanese version)

Three key points.

  • Your records are not sent to the developer. They are kept on your device, in Apple Health, and in your own iCloud. Where they are stored depends on the type of data.
  • The developer receives your Pro purchase status and limited categories of app usage, such as screens opened and steps in the purchase flow. It does not include workout or body measurement values. You can turn off sending usage data in Settings.

This Policy sets out how personal information and user information are handled in the iOS app "Progress Note - 筋トレ記録&プログラム管理" (the "App") provided by Yura Mizutani (the "Developer").

1. Data the App Handles

1-1. Where records are stored

The Developer does not receive the values of the following records.

TypeStorage location

The display cache of measurements retrieved from Apple Health is kept in memory. For manual entries whose saving to HealthKit has been confirmed, the App does not keep a duplicate copy of the quantity and instead keeps reference information. Values that cannot be confirmed, and data set aside during migration, are kept on your device so that they are not lost.

The on-device area for health data is excluded from the OS's automatic backups. Before changing devices or deleting the App, please save your data using the App's free export feature. Exported files are not encrypted and contain health data. Please choose where to save them and with whom to share them.

Syncing of Apple Health between devices depends on your Apple Account, OS settings, permissions, and sync status. The App's own body measurements are not synced automatically; transfer them using a file. iCloud data already saved by older versions is not automatically deleted merely by switching to the new version.

To improve the App and to review pricing and the scope of free features, the App sends the types of events, such as screen usage and steps in the purchase flow, to the analytics service Aptabase. Only the items in the table below are sent; they contain no information identifying who you are and none of the contents of your records.

Things that did not workThe type of the first technical failure that occurred when saving a record, purchasing, restoring, sharing, or importing

Every event includes whether you are on Free or Pro, rough categories of the week you started using the App and the number of days elapsed, the category of your answer to "where you heard about the App" (or "not yet" before you answer), and the App version. Aptabase also records the time sent, OS and its version, language, device model (e.g. iPhone 13), and the country/region derived from network information. For duplicate detection, a single-use number is attached to each report.

Record values such as exercise names, weights, reps, estimated 1RM, body weight, body fat percentage, body measurements, and heart rate; the number of days recorded or the number of sets; and the dates and times that workout and body measurement records refer to are not sent to usage analytics. Your name, email address, Apple Account, the UUID used for purchase management, device identifiers, and advertising identifiers are not sent to analytics either. No user ID that identifies you over a long period is attached.

Turning off "Send usage data" in Settings stops sending from that point on. Events that occur while it is off are never sent later in bulk. Purchase management (1-4) is required for the purchase features and is not covered by this setting. Data from test and development versions is sent to a location separate from production statistics.

1-4. Purchase information

If you purchase Pro, the purchase status (which product, when it was purchased, and until when it is valid) is held by the subscription management service RevenueCat. It is used to aggregate and manage purchase information. Checking eligibility for paid features and restoring purchases are performed by the App based on Apple's purchase information.

This data is linked to a random UUID generated by the App. The RevenueCat SDK also sends technical information such as the device identifier (IDFV) and the OS and App versions. It does not include your name, email address, or Apple Account information. Payment itself is processed by Apple, and the Developer does not receive payment information such as credit card numbers.

RevenueCat uses the country of purchase and the country derived from the IP address at the time of communication for customer information and statistics linked to the purchase management UUID. According to RevenueCat, the original IP address is not retained after the country is derived. The App does not send your current GPS location or your address. In the App Store "App Privacy" section, this country/region information handled by Aptabase and RevenueCat is shown as "Coarse Location" (purpose: Analytics).

RevenueCat also holds the time of your last connection to the service, linked to the purchase management UUID. This information is used for purchase management and checking usage status; it is not a history of individual screen views or taps, nor does it send the dates and times that training records refer to.

1-5. Importing records using an external AI

In the initial release, the entry point for importing programs from an AI or a file is not provided. In the records data import, to convert paper records, photos, screenshots, and similar materials into data, you yourself give instructions and the original images, text, etc. to an external AI of your choice. The App never sends records to an external AI automatically. The conversion instructions copied the first time alone do not include your training records or body measurements.

When you ask the AI to correct or add to the imported content, the request text may include the relevant original, import candidates, related record values, your additional notes, and so on. When a correction request is displayed, the App may copy that request text to the clipboard. Sending to an external AI is done by you pasting it into the AI you have chosen.

You choose what to enter into an external AI. For how the information you enter is handled, please check the terms of use and privacy policy of the AI service you use.
